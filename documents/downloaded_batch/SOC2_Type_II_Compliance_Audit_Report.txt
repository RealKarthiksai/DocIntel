SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SCOPE
----------------------------------------------------------------
Independent service auditor PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP examined
the design and operating effectiveness of controls for the
DocIntel platform against the Trust Services Criteria for
Security, Availability, and Confidentiality.

Auditor's Opinion: UNQUALIFIED. Controls were suitably designed
and operated effectively throughout the period, except as noted
in Section 2.

SECTION 2: TESTING RESULTS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Control Area                   Sample    Exceptions   Result
-----------------------------  ------    ----------   ----------
CC6.1 FIDO2 MFA for admins       25          0         Effective
CC6.2 User access provisioning   40          0         Effective
CC6.3 Access revocation          40          2         Exception
CC7.2 Security monitoring        60          0         Effective
CC8.1 Change management          60          0         Effective
A1.2  Backup and recovery        12          0         Effective
C1.1  Hash-chained audit ledger  60          0         Effective

Notable Observations
  - FIDO2 hardware-key MFA was enforced for 100% of privileged
    accounts; no password-only administrative access was found.
  - Hash ledger integrity tests recomputed chain digests across 60
    sampled days and found no gaps, reorderings, or alterations.
  - Exception: 2 of 40 terminated users retained access for 3 and
    5 days beyond the 24-hour revocation standard. No evidence of
    inappropriate access was identified.

Management Response
  Automated HRIS-to-IdP deprovisioning was deployed on
  November 14, 2026 and now revokes access within 1 hour of
  termination.
