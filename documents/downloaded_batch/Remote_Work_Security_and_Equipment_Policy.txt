SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SCOPE
----------------------------------------------------------------
This policy defines the security controls and equipment
entitlements for employees working remotely, including hybrid and
remote-first roles. It applies to all company-issued devices and
any personal device that accesses corporate data.

SECTION 2: EQUIPMENT AND TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Equipment Stipend
    - One-time home office setup stipend: $1,500
      (desk, chair, monitor, peripherals).
    - Annual refresh allowance: $600.
    - Monthly internet reimbursement: up to $60.
    - Company laptops are issued by IT; stipend funds may not be
      used for computing devices.

2.2 Device Requirements
    - All laptops must be enrolled in Mobile Device Management
      (MDM) before first use.
    - Full-disk encryption (FileVault or BitLocker) enforced.
    - Endpoint detection agent installed and reporting; OS patches
      applied within 14 days of release (critical: 72 hours).
    - Screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.
    - Local administrator rights are disabled by default.

2.3 Network Requirements
    - All access to internal systems must use the company
      WireGuard VPN with device-certificate authentication.
    - Split tunneling is disabled for devices accessing Restricted
      data.
    - Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN active.
    - Home routers must use WPA3 or WPA2-AES with a non-default
      administrator password.

SECTION 3: GOVERNANCE & ENFORCEMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------
    - Non-compliant devices are automatically quarantined from the
      network after 24 hours' notice.
    - Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the Security
      Operations Center within 2 hours.
    - Employees must not process Restricted data in public spaces
      or on shared household devices.
    - Equipment purchased with stipend funds remains company
      property for 24 months, subject to pro-rated repayment upon
      voluntary separation.
    - Exceptions require written approval from the CISO.

Review cycle: annual. Next review: December 2027.
