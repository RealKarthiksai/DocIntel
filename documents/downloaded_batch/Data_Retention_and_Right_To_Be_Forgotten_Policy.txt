SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SCOPE
----------------------------------------------------------------
This policy sets how long Northbridge retains personal and
business data and how we honor erasure requests under GDPR
Article 17 ("right to be forgotten") and similar laws. It applies
to all employees, contractors, systems, and processors handling
personal data of individuals in the EU, UK, and other regulated
regions.

SECTION 2: RETENTION SCHEDULE AND ERASURE WORKFLOW
----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Retention Schedule
    Record Type                      Retention Period
    -------------------------------  -----------------------------
    Financial and tax records        7 years from fiscal year end
    Customer contracts               7 years after contract end
    Employee records                 6 years after termination
    Recruiting data (unsuccessful)   12 months
    Application and access logs      400 days
    Customer document content        Per contract; deleted within
                                     30 days of termination
    Marketing consent records        Life of consent plus 3 years

2.2 Erasure Request Workflow
    1. Intake: Request received via privacy portal or
       privacy@northbridge.example; logged within 1 business day.
    2. Identity verification: completed within 5 business days.
    3. Scoping: Privacy Office maps data across production,
       analytics, backups, and processors.
    4. Legal review: Check exemptions under Article 17(3), such as
       legal claims, regulatory obligations, or legal holds.
    5. Execution: Erase or irreversibly anonymize data; instruct
       processors to do the same.
    6. Confirmation: Written response to the requester within 30
       days (extendable by up to 2 months for complex requests,
       with notice).

2.3 Technical Notes
    - Backups are purged on their normal rotation (maximum 35
      days); restored data is re-erased before use.
    - The audit ledger stores only salted hashes, so erasure of the
      source record cryptographically severs the link without
      breaking the hash chain.

SECTION 3: GOVERNANCE & CONSTRAINTS
----------------------------------------------------------------
    - Active legal holds suspend deletion; the Legal department
      must document the basis and review it quarterly.
    - Erasure requests are never denied without written reasons
      and information on the right to complain to a supervisory
      authority.
    - Privacy Office metrics are reported quarterly to the Audit
      Committee; the 30-day response SLA target is 98%.
    - Annual training is mandatory for all staff with access to
      personal data.
