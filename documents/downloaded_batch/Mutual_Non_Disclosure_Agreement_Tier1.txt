MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT
================================================================
Agreement No.:    NDA-2027-0215
Parties:          Northbridge Global Technologies, Inc.
                  Vantage Ridge Partners, LP
Department:       Legal
Author:           Rachel Kim, Associate General Counsel
Effective Date:   February 1, 2027
Classification:   RESTRICTED
================================================================

SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SCOPE
----------------------------------------------------------------
The parties intend to evaluate a potential strategic transaction
("Project Halcyon") and will exchange non-public information
during due diligence. This Agreement sets the terms under which
each party may use and must protect that information.

Term: Two (2) years from the Effective Date. Confidentiality
obligations survive for five (5) years after termination; trade
secrets remain protected for as long as they qualify as such.

SECTION 2: CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION & OBLIGATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Definition. "Confidential Information" means all financial,
    technical, customer, product-roadmap, and personnel data
    disclosed in any form, whether or not marked confidential,
    including the existence and status of Project Halcyon.

2.2 Permitted Use. The Receiving Party shall use Confidential
    Information solely to evaluate the transaction.

2.3 Standard of Care. The Receiving Party shall protect
    Confidential Information with at least the same care it uses
    for its own sensitive data, and no less than reasonable care.

2.4 Exclusions. Obligations do not apply to information that is
    (a) publicly available through no fault of the Receiving
    Party, (b) already known without restriction, (c)
    independently developed, or (d) lawfully received from a
    third party.

2.5 IP Protection. No license or ownership right is granted.
    All improvements derived from a party's Confidential
    Information remain that party's property.

SECTION 3: GOVERNANCE & REMEDIES
----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Access Controls. Disclosure is limited to named personnel on
    Schedule A (maximum 12 individuals per party) who are bound by
    written confidentiality duties. Data room access is logged.
3.2 Compelled Disclosure. The Receiving Party shall give prompt
    written notice before disclosing under legal compulsion and
    shall cooperate with any request for a protective order.
3.3 Return or Destruction. Within 15 business days of written
    request, all materials shall be returned or destroyed, with an
    officer's certificate of compliance.
3.4 Equitable Relief. Breach may cause irreparable harm; the
    disclosing party may seek injunctive relief without posting
    bond.
3.5 Governing Law. State of Delaware.
