SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SCOPE
----------------------------------------------------------------
This forecast projects recurring revenue, gross margin, and cloud
cost of goods sold (COGS) for the quarter ending March 31, 2027.
It covers all subscription product lines (Platform, Analytics,
and Compliance Cloud) across North America, EMEA, and APAC.

Headline outcome: Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) is forecast to
grow from $84.2M to $91.8M, a net increase of $7.6M (9.0%) in the
quarter. Recognized quarterly revenue is projected at $22.0M with
a blended gross margin of 72.0%.
