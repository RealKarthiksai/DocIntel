SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SCOPE
----------------------------------------------------------------
Helix Data Services provides managed data labeling and OCR
processing for DocIntel. Annual spend is $640,000. The vendor
accesses Confidential documents and is therefore classified
Tier 1 (critical). This assessment follows NIST SP 800-161 Rev. 1
cyber supply chain risk management guidance.

Overall Result: 77% (MODERATE RISK). Approved with conditions.

SECTION 2: SCORING MATRIX
----------------------------------------------------------------
Scale: 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent). Weighted score = weight x score.

-----------------------------  ------   -----   --------
Cybersecurity Controls           30       4       120
Supply Chain Integrity           20       3        60
Financial Stability              15       4        60
Regulatory Compliance            15       5        75

Data Privacy                     10       4        40
                               ------           --------
Total                           100               385 / 500

Risk Tiers: 80% or above = Low; 60-79% = Moderate; below 60% =
High.

Key Findings
  - Strengths: SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified; encryption
    in transit and at rest verified.
  - Gap 1: Fourth-party subcontractors (2 offshore annotation
    teams) lack documented security reviews.
  - Gap 2: DR recovery time objective is 24 hours versus
    Northbridge's required 8 hours.

SECTION 3: GOVERNANCE & CONDITIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------
    - Conditions of approval: close Gap 1 within 90 days and Gap 2
      within 180 days; failure triggers review of the contract.
    - Helix must notify Northbridge of security incidents within
      48 hours and of subcontractor changes within 30 days.
    - Reassessment frequency for Tier 1 vendors with Moderate risk:
      every 12 months, with continuous security-rating monitoring.
    - Approval required from the CISO and the Procurement Director
      for spend above $250,000.
