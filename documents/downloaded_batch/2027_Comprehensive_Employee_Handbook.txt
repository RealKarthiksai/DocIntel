SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SCOPE
----------------------------------------------------------------
This handbook applies to all full-time and part-time employees
worldwide. It summarizes our work model, leave entitlements, and
wellbeing programs for 2027. Where local law provides greater
protection, local law prevails.

SECTION 2: POLICY DETAILS
----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Hybrid Work Schedule
    - Core in-office days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday for roles
      designated "Hybrid" (approx. 62% of workforce).
    - Core collaboration hours: 10:00-15:00 local time.
    - "Remote-First" roles require an approved Remote Work
      Agreement (see HR-POL-2027-014).
    - Managers may approve up to 10 fully remote days per year
      outside the standard schedule.

2.2 Parental Leave (fully paid)
    - Primary caregiver: 20 weeks at 100% of base salary.
    - Secondary caregiver: 12 weeks at 100% of base salary.
    - Leave may be taken within 12 months of birth or placement
      and applies equally to adoption and surrogacy.

2.3 Wellness Recharge Days
    - Four company-wide paid recharge days in 2027:
      Feb 19, May 21, Aug 20, and Nov 19.
    - Annual wellness stipend: $900, reimbursed against receipts.

2.4 Paid Time Off
    - Flexible PTO with a recommended minimum of 15 days.
    - 11 paid company holidays per year in addition to PTO.

SECTION 3: GOVERNANCE & COMPLIANCE
----------------------------------------------------------------
    - Leave requests must be submitted through the HRIS at least
      30 days in advance for planned leave.
    - Managers must complete anti-harassment and unconscious bias
      training within 60 days of hire and annually thereafter.
    - Concerns can be reported to HR, the Ethics Hotline
      (available 24x7, anonymous), or the Legal department.
      Retaliation is strictly prohibited.
    - The handbook is not a contract of employment. Northbridge
      may amend it with 30 days' notice, except where changes are
      required by law.

Acknowledgment of receipt is required within 14 days of issue.
