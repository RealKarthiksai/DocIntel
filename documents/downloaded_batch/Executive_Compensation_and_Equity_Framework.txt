SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SCOPE
----------------------------------------------------------------
This framework sets C-suite base salary benchmarks, annual
incentive targets, and long-term equity design for FY2027. Targets
are positioned at the 60th percentile of a 18-company peer group
selected by independent consultant Pearl Meyer.

SECTION 2: COMPENSATION BENCHMARKS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Role                 Base Salary   Target Bonus   Target LTI (PSU/RSU)
-------------------  -----------   ------------   --------------------
Chief Executive Off. $  750,000       120%           $3,500,000
Chief Financial Off. $  520,000       80%            $1,400,000
Chief Technology Off.$  540,000       80%            $1,500,000
Chief Legal Officer  $  480,000       70%            $1,000,000
Chief People Officer $  430,000       70%            $  850,000

Long-Term Incentive Mix
  - 60% Performance Stock Units (PSUs), 40% Restricted Stock
    Units (RSUs).
  - PSUs vest after a 3-year performance period based on relative
    Total Shareholder Return (50%) and ARR growth (50%).
  - Payout range: 0% to 200% of target; threshold at 25th
    percentile TSR.
  - RSUs vest ratably over 4 years (25% annually).

Annual Bonus Metrics: Revenue 40%, Operating Margin 30%, Net
Revenue Retention 20%, Strategic/ESG Objectives 10%.

SECTION 3: GOVERNANCE & CLAWBACK
----------------------------------------------------------------
  - Clawback: Incentive compensation is subject to recovery for
    36 months following payment if the Company restates its
    financial statements, regardless of fault, in accordance
    with SEC Rule 10D-1 and NYSE listing standards. Recovery
    also applies to misconduct causing material harm.
  - Stock ownership guidelines: CEO 6x base salary; other
    executives 3x base salary, to be met within 5 years.
  - Hedging and pledging of Company stock is prohibited.
  - All changes require approval by the Compensation Committee;
    amendments to equity plans require Board approval.
  - Access to this document is limited to Committee members, the
    CPO, the CLO, and the external consultant.
