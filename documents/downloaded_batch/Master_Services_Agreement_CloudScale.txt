================================================================

SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SCOPE
----------------------------------------------------------------
This Master Services Agreement governs Provider's delivery of
managed cloud hosting, disaster recovery, and 24x7 operations
support to Customer. Initial term is 36 months from the
Effective Date, renewing for successive 12-month periods unless
either party gives 90 days' written notice of non-renewal.

Annual Fees: $2,400,000, invoiced quarterly in advance, Net 30.

SECTION 2: SERVICE LEVELS & COMMERCIAL TERMS
----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Availability SLA ("Four Nines")
    Provider shall maintain 99.99% monthly uptime for production
    services, equal to no more than 4.38 minutes of unplanned
    downtime per month (about 52.6 minutes per year). Scheduled
    maintenance in agreed windows is excluded.

2.2 Service Credits (percentage of monthly fees)
      Uptime >= 99.90% and < 99.99% ........ 10%
      Uptime >= 99.00% and < 99.90% ........ 25%
      Uptime < 99.00% ....................... 50%
    Credits are the sole remedy for SLA failures except under
    Section 3.3 (chronic failure).

2.3 Incident Response
    Severity-1 incidents: 15-minute response, 4-hour resolution
    target, with root-cause analysis delivered within 5 business
    days.

SECTION 3: GOVERNANCE, LIABILITY & LEGAL TERMS
----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Limitation of Liability
    Each party's aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid or
    payable in the 12 months preceding the claim ($2,400,000).
    The cap does not apply to confidentiality breaches, data
    protection violations, gross negligence, or indemnified
    third-party IP claims.

3.2 Data Protection
    Provider shall encrypt Customer data at rest (AES-256) and in
    transit (TLS 1.2 or higher), and shall notify Customer of any
    security incident within 48 hours of discovery.

3.3 Chronic Failure
    Customer may terminate without penalty if uptime falls below
    99.90% in any three months of a rolling six-month period.

3.4 Governing Law & Venue
    This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
    Delaware. Disputes shall be heard exclusively in the state or
    federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware.
