SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SCOPE
----------------------------------------------------------------
This runbook defines how on-call engineers detect, triage,
mitigate, and communicate during production incidents. It covers
Severity 1 and Severity 2 events for the DocIntel platform and
supporting services.

Severity Definitions
  Sev-1: Full outage, data loss risk, or security breach
         affecting customers.
  Sev-2: Major degradation affecting more than 10% of traffic or a
         key workflow.
  Sev-3: Minor impact with an available workaround.

SECTION 2: TRIAGE AND RESPONSE PROCEDURES
----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Sev-1 Timeline
    T+0 min   Alert fires; primary on-call acknowledges within
              5 minutes or escalates automatically.
    T+10 min  Incident Commander (IC) assigned; war-room channel
              #inc-YYYYMMDD-N opened.
    T+15 min  First internal status update posted.
    T+30 min  Customer-facing status page updated; updates every
              30 minutes thereafter.
    T+4 hrs   Target mitigation. Escalate to VP Engineering if
              not mitigated.

2.2 Triage Checklist
    1. Check recent deployments (last 2 hours) and feature flags.
    2. Review dashboards: error rate, latency p95, saturation.
    3. Inspect pod health:
         kubectl get pods -n prod --field-selector=status.phase!=Running
    4. Review logs for the failing service:
         kubectl logs deploy/api-gateway -n prod --since=30m

2.3 Rollback Commands
    - Kubernetes deployment:
         kubectl rollout undo deployment/api-gateway -n prod
         kubectl rollout status deployment/api-gateway -n prod
    - Helm release:
         helm history docproc -n prod
         helm rollback docproc <REVISION> -n prod --wait
    - Database failover (Postgres HA):
         patronictl -c /etc/patroni.yml failover --candidate pg-02

SECTION 3: GOVERNANCE & CONSTRAINTS
----------------------------------------------------------------
    - Database failover and rollbacks that may cause data loss
      require IC approval and a second engineer's confirmation.
    - A blameless post-incident review is due within 5 business
      days for Sev-1 and 10 for Sev-2; action items are tracked to
      closure in Jira.
    - On-call rotation: 7-day shifts, primary and secondary,
      with compensation of $1,200 per week plus time-off in lieu.
    - Security incidents must also be reported to the SOC within
      15 minutes and follow IR-SEC-2027-001.
