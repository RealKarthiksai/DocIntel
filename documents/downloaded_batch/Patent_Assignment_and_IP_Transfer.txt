assign to Northbridge Global Technologies, Inc. ("Assignee") all
right, title, and interest in the invention titled:

  "Hash-Chained Tamper-Evident Audit Ledger for Document
   Workflow Systems"

Provisional Application No. 63/812,447, filed December 19, 2026.
Internal Disclosure Reference: IDF-2026-0338.

The invention is designated a classified trade secret until the
non-provisional filing publishes, expected no earlier than 18
months from the priority date.

SECTION 2: TECHNICAL SUMMARY & CONSIDERATION
----------------------------------------------------------------
Claims Overview
  Claim 1: A method of recording document events in which each
           ledger entry stores a SHA-256 digest of the prior entry
           concatenated with the current event payload.
  Claim 2: Periodic anchoring of the chain head to an independent
           write-once store, enabling third-party verification.
  Claim 3: A verification service that detects insertion,
           deletion, or reordering of entries in O(n) time.
